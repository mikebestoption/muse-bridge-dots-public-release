    Run tests with Dot

    The packet includes the current conversation as the material to test, with What to check / Where / Expected result, and asks Dot to reply in CLAIM | RESULT | EVIDENCE lines.
    After Dot answers in ChatGPT: use “Import from clipboard” or import Dot's file from the Inbox, then choose “Log as Dot test result” when asked — verdicts are written to the chat and to test-log.md.

    Settings

    Stored only on this PC, encrypted by your operating system. Never sent anywhere except api.meta.ai.
    New Night Queue jobs are flagged NEEDS DOT AUDIT by default (you can toggle each job in the queue list).
    When the vault auto-locks, fills stop and Night Queue fill jobs park as "waiting on you" until you unlock it again.
    Before the app types anything (paste to an app, fill a login, fill a 2FA code) in the Night Queue, it waits until you have not touched the mouse or keyboard for this long. While you are using the PC, hands steps wait — they never type over you. Steps you approve by hand only ever wait for a 3-second hands-off countdown.
    If you are still using the PC after waiting this long, the hands step parks as "waiting on you" (reason: user active on the PC) and the Night Queue moves on with its other jobs. If the screen is locked or the session is switched to another user, hands steps park immediately instead of waiting.
    Muse Bridge — updates come from the public GitHub Releases feed. Downloaded updates install only when you click Restart to update; your key, Vault and history are never touched.
