🎬 Teach from video — turn a recording into an SOP
Paste a Vimeo link (that's what you use), or drop / pick a video file (mp4, webm, mov — up to 500 MB). The app samples frames from the recording and asks Muse Spark to write an SOP: exactly what to click, open, and do, step by step. A Vimeo link on its own saves the link + public title only — the recording itself is analyzed from the video file, or from the link when a Vimeo access token is saved in Settings. An SOP can then become a Loop.
